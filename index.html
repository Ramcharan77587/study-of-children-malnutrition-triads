Empirical Key Takeaways

• Sanitation & Literacy: Exhibit the strongest protective linear gradients ($r = -0.50$ to $-0.58$) against stunting and underweight.

• The Water Paradox: Improved water access exhibits near-zero linear correlation ($r = -0.037$) due to a 97% statistical ceiling effect and water purity decoupling.

• Adolescent Marriage: Highest vulnerability driver ($r = +0.481$ with underweight), perpetuating intergenerational growth faltering.
