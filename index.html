Spatial Discovery

The Stunting–Wasting Duality

Stunting (33.5% mean) and Wasting (18.5% mean) are statistically independent (r = +0.224, R² < 0.05). Stunting concentrates in the eastern Gangetic plains, while acute wasting clusters heavily across the western industrialized corridor (Gujarat and Maharashtra).

Econometric Drivers

Top Protective Levers

Improved Sanitation (r = -0.583 with Underweight, -0.503 with Stunting) and Female Literacy (r = -0.545 with Underweight, -0.539 with Stunting) emerge as the two primary multi-sectoral protective factors across India's 706 districts.

Phenomenon Resolution

The Water Access Paradox

Improved water access shows near-zero correlation (r = -0.037) with stunting. Our audit reveals a statistical ceiling effect (97% median access) and microbiological quality decoupling at point-of-use (carriage and open vessel storage contamination).
